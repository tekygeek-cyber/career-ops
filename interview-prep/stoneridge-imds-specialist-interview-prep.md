## Technical Interview Questions and Answers

### What is IMDS?

IMDS stands for International Material Data System. It is the automotive industry database used to collect, maintain and report material composition information for parts and assemblies. It helps OEMs and suppliers meet environmental and regulatory requirements.

---

### Why is IMDS important?

IMDS is important because automotive customers need accurate material composition data to confirm compliance with regulations such as ELV, REACH and RoHS. It also supports recycling, restricted substance monitoring and customer approval processes.

---

### What do you check when reviewing an MDS?

I would check:

- Correct part number and description
- Supplier details
- Material classification
- Substance breakdown
- Percentage totals
- Basic substance accuracy
- GADSL or declarable substance flags
- REACH / SVHC relevance
- Customer-specific rules
- Alignment with BOM and drawing information

---

### What would you do if an MDS is rejected?

First, I would review the rejection reason carefully. Then I would identify whether the issue is related to structure, material classification, missing substance data, incorrect part information, customer rule violation or supplier data quality. After that, I would correct the issue internally if possible or contact the supplier with clear instructions. I would track the correction until the MDS is accepted.

---

### What is PPAP and how does IMDS connect to it?

PPAP stands for Production Part Approval Process. It confirms that a supplier can consistently produce parts that meet customer requirements. IMDS is often one of the supporting requirements for PPAP because customers need material compliance confirmation before approving production parts.

---

### What is REACH?

REACH is an EU chemical regulation covering Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals. From an IMDS and supplier compliance perspective, the key focus is usually SVHC monitoring, supplier declarations and communication when substances exceed reporting thresholds.

---

### What is SCIP?

SCIP is the ECHA database for information on substances of concern in articles. Companies supplying articles in the EU need to provide information when articles contain SVHCs above 0.1% weight by weight. IMDS data and supplier declarations can support SCIP reporting.

---

### What is ELV?

ELV stands for End-of-Life Vehicles Directive. It restricts certain hazardous substances in vehicles and supports recyclability and environmental protection. IMDS helps demonstrate compliance with ELV requirements.

---

### What is RoHS?

RoHS restricts hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment. For automotive electronics, RoHS awareness is important because many components may contain electronic materials, solder, coatings or restricted substances.

---

### How do you manage suppliers who delay IMDS submissions?

I would first send a clear request explaining the requirement, deadline and customer impact. If there is no response, I would follow up with specific action points and involve procurement or quality if needed. I would keep a tracker of open items, escalation dates and responsible persons until closure.

---

### What makes a good IMDS Specialist?

A good IMDS Specialist needs attention to detail, understanding of material compliance, ability to work with suppliers, patience in resolving data issues and good communication with quality, engineering, procurement and customer teams. It is a role that requires both technical accuracy and coordination skills.
